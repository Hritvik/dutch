# Define trust, dispute, and support operations

## Question

How can a Member report abuse or an incorrect collaborative change; what self-service correction, Group Admin, support, and incident paths exist; what support access is permitted; and how are privacy, immutable evidence, and financial correction preserved throughout?

## Answer

### Reports and financial disagreements

- A Member may privately report an Expense, Settlement, Activity entry, or Member as incorrect, unauthorized, or abusive, with an optional plain-text explanation. A Report preserves its case-linked evidence but does not automatically change a balance, freeze a Group, notify the reported person, or create public Activity.
- Dutch does not adjudicate who is right about a debt or force a financial correction. Members resolve the record through the established Correct/Void/Restore flows; support explains available evidence and platform policy, but never acts as a debt court.
- Any authorized collaborator may use ordinary correction flows. Group Admins have no hidden financial override beyond those same attributable actions. Support cannot directly edit Ledger entries, impersonate a Member, or apply an untracked database correction.

### Safety and support access

- A Member may create a Safety Block against another Member. It prevents either party from initiating a new Direct Expense/Series or new Group invitation involving the other; it does not erase existing financial evidence, cancel obligations, revoke existing Group access, or prevent authorized correction of historical data.
- Support receives the minimum case-linked evidence through time-limited, audited, role-based access. Reports and case status are private to the reporter and support staff; unrelated Group data is not surfaced.
- For account-takeover or safety incidents, support uses IAM security-lock/session revocation and the established recovery process. Platform-policy enforcement and account actions are themselves auditable; financial history remains immutable.

### Operating boundary

- Support case lifecycle, retention, service targets, and applicable legal obligations are inputs to **Define launch-market, legal, scale, and measurement targets**. They do not weaken the settled privacy, identity, or Ledger boundaries.
