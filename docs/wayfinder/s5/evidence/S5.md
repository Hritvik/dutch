## Implemented

- **Direct Expense** (`com.dutch.ledger.direct`): a one-off Expense outside any
  Group with the same exact payer/share snapshot, deterministic
  largest-debtor/largest-creditor entries, and at least two involved members.
  Entries are grouped by the canonical direct-pair identity
  `direct:<lower MemberId>:<higher MemberId>:<currency>`, independent of who paid.
- **Recurring Series** (`com.dutch.ledger.series`): weekly or monthly cadence in an
  IANA timezone with a due day (monthly clamps to the month length). A series exposes
  one explicit Pending Occurrence per due period and never creates debt by itself.
  EQUAL, PERCENTAGE, and EXACT templates derive exact shares; pause, resume, archive,
  skip, submit, and miss are guarded transitions.
- **Persistence** (`V9`): direct expense/revision/payer/share tables with deferred
  balance constraints and immutability triggers; canonical direct-pair streams reuse
  `obligation_entry` with a `direct_expense_id` source and rebuild the member balance
  projection. Series, payer, participant, and occurrence tables enforce one occurrence
  per series/period.
- **Direct Expense HTTP** (`POST/GET /api/v1/direct-expenses`): an involved member
  creates a Direct Expense with `Idempotency-Key`; the caller must be in the
  payer/share snapshot, every involved Member must be active in the local UMS
  projection, and only involved members can read the expense or its Activity.
- **Series HTTP** (`/api/v1/series`, `/api/v1/groups/{groupId}/series`): create and
  list Group/Direct Series, generate the next Pending Occurrence with `If-Match`,
  list occurrences, skip once, submit once, and pause/resume/archive with version
  guards and durable idempotent replay. Submission builds the normal Group or Direct
  Expense from the template; generation advances `nextPeriodStart` in the same
  transaction. All commands append privacy-filtered Activity.
- **Sync journal** (`V10`, `JdbcSyncJournal`): append-only `member_sync_change` rows
  with an independent per-recipient sequence allocated in the command transaction.
  Financial, Series, occurrence, and Group mutations append an `upsert` with the
  compact resource payload for every authorized recipient; snapshots fold the latest
  non-tombstone row per resource.
- **Sync HTTP**: `GET /api/v1/ledger/sync` returns the caller's ordered page from a
  signed opaque cursor, `POST /api/v1/ledger/sync-snapshots` issues a 15-minute
  recipient-bound snapshot token, and `GET /api/v1/ledger/sync-snapshots/{token}`
  returns the folded projections plus the starting cursor. Tokens are HMAC-signed
  and bind the recipient and sequence; missing, foreign, tampered, or expired
  material is `409 sync_cursor_expired` and reveals nothing.
- **Repositories**: `JdbcDirectExpenseRepository` appends a direct revision, entries,
  stream versions, and projections; `JdbcSeriesRepository` stores templates,
  lifecycle/next-period updates with optimistic version checks, and pending
  occurrence transitions.

- **SQLite projection** (`apps/mobile/src/projection-db.ts`): account-scoped
  `resources`, `tombstones`, `drafts`, and `cursors` tables in WAL mode. A sync page
  and its cursor commit in one transaction; versions order upserts, a tombstone wins,
  and a stale upsert never resurrects it. Switching the active Member wipes the scope
  atomically, and sign-out deletes resources, cursors, drafts, and the binding.
- **Sync coordinator** (`apps/mobile/src/sync-coordinator.ts`): incremental page when a
  cursor exists, a signed snapshot when it is missing or `sync_cursor_expired`, then
  bounded incremental catch-up. Network failure falls back to cached Groups and marks
  Groups as offline.
- **Local Drafts**: settlement payer/payee/amount and correction amount are saved per
  Member in SQLite, restored when the screen reopens, and deleted on acceptance.
- **Mobile clients** (`apps/mobile/src`): a version/tombstone cache reducer applies a
  sync page atomically, never resurrects a tombstoned resource, fails closed on
  `full_resync_required`, and advances the cursor only with its page. Signed sync and
  snapshot clients surface `sync_cursor_expired`; closed Direct Expense and Series
  clients cover create/read/activity, occurrence generation with `If-Match`, skip,
  submit, and status changes. A Direct Expense screen creates a two-member equal split
  from the Home surface. Group detail lists recurring Series and opens a Series screen
  that generates the next occurrence, submits or skips a pending one, and pauses,
  resumes, or archives the template.

  - equal/multi-payer/exact direct entries net to the expected positions,
  - a direct expense uses one canonical stream per pair and rebuilds projections,
  - imbalanced direct revisions are rejected at commit,
  - monthly due days clamp and weekly due dates use the series timezone,
  - a Pending Occurrence carries no expense until submitted,
  - skip/miss never carry an expense; pause/archive generate nothing,
  - one occurrence per series/period is enforced by the database,
  - series lifecycle and next-period updates require the expected version,
  - a Direct Expense HTTP create/read/Activity enforces involvement and replays keys,
  - a Group Series generates exactly one Pending Occurrence and advances its version,
  - occurrence submit creates one Group or Direct Expense and replays it,
  - skip, pause, resume, and archive are one-shot and version-guarded,
  - a non-member cannot read or create a Group Series,
  - each recipient receives an independent journal sequence and only their own rows,
  - snapshots fold the latest upsert and drop a tombstoned resource,
  - a missing, tampered, foreign, or expired cursor is `sync_cursor_expired`,
  - the sync page limit is bounded and a snapshot cursor starts incremental sync.

  Series, and sync paths, the sync schemas, and the `sync_cursor_expired` code.

## Device evidence (Android)

`Medium_Phone_API_36.0` with the dev session and local mock API:

```bash
maestro --device emulator-5554 test apps/mobile/.maestro/direct-expense.yaml
maestro --device emulator-5554 test apps/mobile/.maestro/series.yaml
```

The flows sign in, create a two-member "Dinner" Direct Expense (two 50.00 INR shares,
the obligation, and the canonical `direct:…` pair stream), and create a monthly "Rent"
Series, generate its 2026-11-01 occurrence, submit it, and confirm it in the Group's
Series list → `android-direct-expense.png`, `android-series-submitted.png`,
`android-series-group.png`.

Offline and recovery evidence on the same emulator:

- `offline-cache-prime.yaml` signs in online and opens Groups, populating the SQLite
  Group projection → `android-online-groups.png`.
- With airplane mode and Wi-Fi/data disabled, `offline-cache-read.yaml` navigates back
  and opens Groups again: the app shows "Offline · showing the last cached projection"
  and the cached "Flat 12" → `android-offline-groups.png`.
- On a fresh mock, `sync-recovery.yaml` presents the stored cursor, receives
  `409 sync_cursor_expired`, falls back to a snapshot, and shows
  "Synced 1 change" → `android-sync-recovered.png`.
- `sign-out.yaml` signs out; pulling the SQLite database before and after shows
  `resources 1 → 0`, `cursors 1 → 0`, `drafts 0 → 0`, and no active Member
  → `android-signed-out.png`.

These prove the client contract, cache, recovery, and wipe against the mock, not the
live Ledger.
