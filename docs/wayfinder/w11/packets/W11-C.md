# W11-C — Prove idempotency and fault handling

## Objective

Prove duplicate delivery, conflicting payload reuse, concurrent consumption and restart behaviour against PostgreSQL, and record the evidence artifact.

## Outputs and ownership

Own the end-to-end integration test and the W-11 evidence artifact. Do not edit the contracts.

## Acceptance

- Sequential, concurrent and post-restart delivery converge on one profile/mapping/version.
- A conflicting reuse of an inbox identity is quarantined/failed without a second effect.
- The mapping event validates against the pinned Dutch schema.
- The UMS database remains independent of IAM.
