# Shared Identity Platform

The portfolio-wide Identity Platform is owned by the dedicated IAM repository, not Dutch.

Canonical artifacts:

- [Identity Platform Wayfinder](../../../iam/docs/identity-platform/wayfinder/map.md)
- [Identity Platform context](../../../iam/docs/identity-platform/IAM-CONTEXT.md)
- [Implementation handoff](../../../iam/docs/identity-platform/wayfinder/issues/15-define-implementation-handoff.md)
- [Cloud reconciliation ticket](../../../iam/docs/identity-platform/wayfinder/issues/16-reconcile-cloud-solution.md)

Dutch owns only its App Registration inputs, client/resource integration, Dutch Member Profiles in UMS, profile-readiness behavior, and Dutch-specific product authorization. Shared identity, verified identifiers, authentication, OAuth/OIDC, sessions, workload identity, and portfolio lifecycle contracts remain IAM-owned.
